## 4. Open dependency — stated, not faked

V001's **full front figure** and **right-side profile** are drawn plates that do not exist anywhere in the approved reference set (the approved master is a portrait plus detail crops). Reproducing them would mean generating new character artwork — outside Assistant VA's authority and precisely the route that produced the invented right-side plate in V001. V002 therefore does not show them; both zones are hatched and labelled `absent from the approved set`. If the CD wants a full figure or a right profile on the sheet, that is a new-artwork task for Original VA with approval before it enters a breakdown.
