## B. Open issues the consultant should know about

### B.1 Claims with no citation in the package
- **Asafo flags (Cape Coast, Ghana):** one MA thesis (AID11) now supports the existence and documentation of Cape Coast flags. Pole mounting and funerary use are not established by it. (Updated in v1.1.)
- **Bongo grave posts (Sudan):** stated from general knowledge. Needs a citation.
- **Vodun and Yoruba white-cloth trees:** the Yoruba practice is reported in a search snippet (APAS-YOR; authors not retrieved). The vodun comparison rests only on a Tier 4 Brazilian text. Both remain unverified for this package. (Updated in v1.1.)

### B.2 Single-lineage claims
- **Obimo two-chamber grave, Ozo burial rites, and the Igirigi/BJMCMR/Etefia passages** all trace to the same underlying account (Ugwuoke 1998; Onu & Etefia 2019). They are one lineage of reporting, not several independent confirmations. The research package has been corrected to say so. Any reliance on these claims needs the original.

### B.3 Possibly duplicated sources
- IRB00 (Canadian IRB, 2000) and ECO00 (ecoi.net, 2000) may be the same document. Check before counting them as two sources.

### B.4 Secondary-only anchors
Several central Tier 2 works have been recorded from prior knowledge and not retrieved in this session. The package marks them NOT RETRIEVED. The most important to check first are Basden (1921/1966), Uchendu (1965), Metuh (1981), Cole (1982), Onwuejeogwu (1981), Henderson (1972), Shaw (1970), Ottenberg (1975), and Amadiume (1987).

### B.5 Items the consultant may find sensitive
- The Igirigi and related passages describe the disposal of unmarried men's and children's corpses in the "evil forest" in some places (C-06). Included as Q46; discuss only if appropriate.
- The widowhood material (C-29) describes practices that affect women's health and dignity. It is reported as variable and not Igbo-specific in one source. It is included for context, not as a depiction to reproduce.

### B.6 Visual issues outside the cultural review
The board's known visual issues (sparse poles; thin, low-contrast strips) are not cultural questions. They are not addressed by this pack and no decision is made on them.

### B.7 Decisions that belong to the Creative Director
- Whether the world is Igbo-inspired, Igbo-attributed, or a separate invented culture with an Igbo-inspired visual language. This changes the risk level and is not a research decision.
- Whether the burial-cloth elements are kept, redesigned, or dropped. Not decided here.
- Whether the newer foreground strip carries a recent-death reading. Not decided here.
- Whether to proceed to visual revision. The research recommends holding revision of the burial-cloth elements until consultant review is complete (see `RESEARCH/C`, Part 6). That recommendation is a research input, not a decision.
