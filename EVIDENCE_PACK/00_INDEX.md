## What this pack is

A set of documents that lets a qualified Igbo cultural consultant see the evidence behind the research package in `RESEARCH/`, the specific questions the research could not settle, and a place to record answers.

## What this pack is not

- It is not a consultation. Desk research does not replace a living custodian.
- It makes **no design decisions**. It does not approve, reject, modify or redirect the artwork. Creative decisions remain with the Creative Director.
- It does not commission artwork, alter Art Bible assets or asset statuses, change `DECISIONS.md`, or touch Google Drive. No Drive upload or move was performed.

## Labels used

DOCUMENTED · CORROBORATED · REGIONALLY SPECIFIC · DISPUTED · INFERRED · SPECULATIVE · FICTIONAL. Verification status is shown separately from the evidence label (see `02` and `03`).

## Suggested reading order for a consultant

1. `01_Consultant_Brief.md` (10 minutes)
2. `04_Board_Element_Evidence_Map.md` (to see which board text the questions refer to)
3. `05_Consultant_Response_Sheet.csv` (to record answers as you go)
4. `02` and `03` only where you want to check a specific source

## Important limits

- Several key sources were located only through secondary summaries or search snippets. They are marked as such. Some full citations are unverified. See `03`.
- The research found no documented pre-colonial Igbo pole-and-cloth funerary practice. That is a finding about the sources searched, not proof that no community ever used such a form.
- Igbo practice varies by community, period, gender and status. No answer here should be read as the practice of all Igbo people.
