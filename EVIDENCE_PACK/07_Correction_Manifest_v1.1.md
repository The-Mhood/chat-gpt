## 5. Non-Igbo comparisons and Aidoo (2011)

- **Aidoo (2011)** added to G and log 03 as **AID11**. MA thesis, KNUST, *Documentation of the Fante Asafo flags since the year 2000 and their socio-cultural significance*.
  - **Scope:** Cape Coast (Fante) asafo flags only. **Not Igbo.**
  - **Retrieved (repository record and abstract):** residents recognise the flags but often cannot interpret them; no written documentation was found at the posts visited; documentation is recommended.
  - **Not established:** pole mounting, funerary use, any link to Igbo practice. The full PDF was **not read** (page fetch failed).
  - **Not used:** the acjol.org claim that Aidoo dates the flags to the early eighteenth century. That is a secondary article's statement and is not verified against the thesis.
- **Bongo grave posts (Sudan):** no source in the package. Unverified. Not Igbo.
- **Vodun cloth-wrapped trees (Ewe, Jeje, Brazil):** Tier 4 text only. Unverified. Not Igbo.
- **Yoruba white cloth on sacred trees (OAAJIS_1_1_8):** search snippet only. Authors and title not retrieved. Non-Igbo. Earlier detail "northeastern Yoruba" and "Tubi 2015" was **not** in any retrieved text. Removed.
- Comparative statements in the brief, the conclusions (B, C) and the risk register (E R-22) are qualified to match these statuses.
