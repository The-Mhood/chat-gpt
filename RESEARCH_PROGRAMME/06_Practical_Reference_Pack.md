# 06 — Practical Reference Pack (Deliverable 6)

For the Visual Agent and engineering team. Working date 2026-11-17. RESEARCH ONLY. Recommendations to the Creative Director; none of this approves an asset.

**How to read this pack.** Each item carries an evidence label (DOCUMENTED, CORROBORATED, REGIONALLY SPECIFIC, DISPUTED, INFERRED, SPECULATIVE, FICTIONAL) and a source code (file 02). **Evidence is not a licence to use.** Restricted items are marked and must not be used without a CD ruling and consultation. Everything not sourced is either labelled FICTIONAL or marked "no source".

## 0. Rules for use

1. Name the community or region when you use a documented practice. Do not write "Igbo" for a regional detail.
2. Do not borrow one community's custom and apply it across the game.
3. Do not present fiction as documented. Use the disclaimer where the world is shown.
4. Do not use any item marked RESTRICTED. Do not reproduce ritual sequences.
5. Where a source is only Tier 4 (blog, wiki, news, aggregator), it is a lead, not a fact.
6. Where the CD has not decided, do not decide. Flag and ask.

## G. Disclaimers and labels (use on any item that is not documented)

- **Fiction label (for any environment or object without a sourced basis):** "Fictional. Inspired by [region], not a depiction of any documented practice."
- **Regional label (for any documented item):** "[Community or region], [date or period]. Source: [code]."
- **Restricted label:** "Restricted. Not for use without written CD approval and consultation."

## H. Do not

- Do not write "Igbo" for a regional detail.
- Do not generalise one community's burial, mourning or cloth practice to all Igbo.
- Do not use Tier 4 sources for a historical or cultural claim.
- Do not use Igbo-Ukwu dates or identity as settled.
- Do not use silk, Nguni shields, Yoruba swords, Igala forms or Senegalese funerals as Igbo.
- Do not depict burial sequences, bad-death or widowhood rites, ikenga, ichi scarification, ozo regalia, masquerades, nsibidi, mbari, uli shrine imagery or Ekpe items.
- Do not caption any board as an Igbo funerary marker.
