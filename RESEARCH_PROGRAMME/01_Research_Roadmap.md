## 1. Principles that set the order

1. **Research serves a decision.** Each item below names the decision it informs. Research that does not inform a pending decision is put in "can wait".
2. **Research cannot clear what only a consultant or the CD can clear.** Burial-associated imagery, restricted topics and living-community questions are gated, not solved by desk work.
3. **Use what is already documented before searching more.** Several production items (the ekwe, the woven shield, the wrapper and isiagu, forked posts) already have enough sourced evidence to proceed with the fix direction given by the review.
4. **Do not let research become a prerequisite for everything.** Fictional elements may proceed when they are labelled as fiction and do not borrow an Igbo practice.

## 5. What research will not do

- It will not clear D9 or any burial-associated board. Only the consultant process and the CD can do that.
- It will not name a shade tree, a palace-guard uniform, a sword type or a ritual object that no source supports.
- It will not treat Igbo-Ukwu as proof of any living practice.
- It will not change any accepted pack entry (RESEARCH/ or EVIDENCE_PACK/).
